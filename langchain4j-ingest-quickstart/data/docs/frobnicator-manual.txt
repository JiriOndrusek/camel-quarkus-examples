Frobnicator X200 - Owner's Manual

Power supply: the Frobnicator X200 ships with a EUROPA-MK1 power supply rated at 12 volts.
Never operate the device with a third-party power supply.

Warranty: the warranty period is twenty four months from the date of purchase.
Keep the original receipt; it is required for any warranty claim.

Safety: never immerse the device in water. The maximum ambient operating temperature
is forty degrees Celsius.

Maintenance: the filter should be replaced every six months. Replacement filters are
available under part number FRB-X200-FLT.
